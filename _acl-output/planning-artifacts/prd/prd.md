**Product Requirements Document (PRD) for Fleet 360 Delivery**

**Project Overview**

Fleet 360 Delivery is a comprehensive logistics and delivery management platform designed to streamline the delivery process for businesses and customers. This greenfield project aims to create a scalable, user-friendly, and feature-rich platform that meets the evolving needs of the logistics industry.

**Product Vision**

Fleet 360 Delivery will be a leading logistics and delivery management platform, providing a seamless experience for businesses and customers through its intuitive interface, real-time tracking, and advanced analytics. Our platform will empower businesses to optimize their delivery operations, reduce costs, and enhance customer satisfaction.

**Product Goals**

1. **Improve Delivery Experience**: Enhance the delivery experience for customers through real-time tracking, estimated delivery times, and notifications.
2. **Optimize Delivery Operations**: Provide businesses with tools to optimize their delivery operations, including route optimization, driver management, and package tracking.
3. **Increase Efficiency**: Streamline delivery processes through automation, reducing manual errors and increasing productivity.
4. **Enhance Customer Engagement**: Offer businesses a platform to engage with customers through personalized communication, surveys, and reviews.

**Target Audience**

1. **Businesses**: E-commerce companies, retailers, and logistics providers seeking to optimize their delivery operations.
2. **Customers**: Individuals receiving packages and deliveries from businesses.
3. **Drivers**: Independent contractors and employees responsible for delivering packages.

**Key Features**

1. **User Management**: Businesses can create and manage user accounts, including administrators, drivers, and customers.
2. **Package Tracking**: Real-time tracking of packages, including estimated delivery times and updates.
3. **Route Optimization**: Businesses can optimize delivery routes to reduce fuel consumption, lower emissions, and increase efficiency.
4. **Driver Management**: Businesses can manage driver schedules, assignments, and performance metrics.
5. **Customer Engagement**: Businesses can engage with customers through personalized communication, surveys, and reviews.
6. **Analytics and Reporting**: Businesses can access detailed analytics and reporting on delivery performance, customer satisfaction, and operational efficiency.
7. **Integration**: Integration with existing systems, including ERP, CRM, and transportation management systems.

**User Stories**

1. **As a business owner, I want to create and manage user accounts for my employees and drivers so that I can control access and permissions.**
	* Acceptance Criteria:
		+ Users can create and manage user accounts.
		+ Users can assign roles and permissions to users.
		+ Users can view and manage user account information.
2. **As a customer, I want to track my package in real-time so that I can stay informed about its delivery status.**
	* Acceptance Criteria:
		+ Customers can track packages in real-time.
		+ Customers can view estimated delivery times and updates.
		+ Customers can receive notifications about package delivery status.
3. **As a driver, I want to view and manage my schedule and assignments so that I can plan my day effectively.**
	* Acceptance Criteria:
		+ Drivers can view and manage their schedule and assignments.
		+ Drivers can receive notifications about new assignments and schedule changes.
		+ Drivers can view and manage their performance metrics.

**Non-Functional Requirements**

1. **Security**: The platform must ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of user data.
2. **Scalability**: The platform must be able to handle a large volume of users and transactions.
3. **Performance**: The platform must provide fast and responsive performance, including page loading times and transaction processing.
4. **Usability**: The platform must provide an intuitive and user-friendly interface for businesses and customers.

**Technical Requirements**

1. **Infrastructure**: The platform will be built on a cloud-based infrastructure, including Amazon Web Services (AWS) or Microsoft Azure.
2. **Programming Languages**: The platform will be built using modern programming languages, including Java, Python, or Node.js.
3. **Database**: The platform will use a relational database management system, including MySQL or PostgreSQL.
4. **APIs**: The platform will expose APIs for integration with existing systems and third-party services.

**Testing and Quality Assurance**

1. **Unit Testing**: The platform will undergo unit testing to ensure individual components function correctly.
2. **Integration Testing**: The platform will undergo integration testing to ensure components work together seamlessly.
3. **System Testing**: The platform will undergo system testing to ensure the entire system functions as expected.
4. **User Acceptance Testing**: The platform will undergo user acceptance testing to ensure it meets business requirements and user expectations.

**Deployment and Maintenance**

1. **Deployment**: The platform will be deployed on a cloud-based infrastructure, including AWS or Microsoft Azure.
2. **Maintenance**: The platform will undergo regular maintenance, including software updates, security patches, and performance optimization.
3. **Backup and Recovery**: The platform will have a backup and recovery plan in place to ensure data integrity and availability.
